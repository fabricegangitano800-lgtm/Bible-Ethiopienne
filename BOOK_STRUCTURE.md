## ILLUSTRATION STYLE GUIDE

### Visual Style
- **Realistic historical** — oil painting feel, not cartoon or abstract
- **Color palette**: warm earth tones (ochre, sienna, gold, deep blue, crimson)
- **Lighting**: dramatic chiaroscuro — divine light as a key element
- **Cultural accuracy**: Ethiopian/Middle Eastern setting, clothing, architecture
- **Consistent faces**: key characters (Moses, David, Jesus, Paul) should look consistent across illustrations
- **No modern elements**: strict historical accuracy

### Ethiopian Elements (throughout)
- Ge'ez script as decorative borders on part title pages
- Ethiopian processional crosses as section dividers
- Manuscript-style illuminated letters for chapter openings
- Colors inspired by Ethiopian church paintings (bright primary colors on dark backgrounds)

### Format
- Scene illustrations: full page, landscape or portrait depending on scene
- Part title pages: illustration + Part title + decorative border
- Print resolution: 300 DPI minimum
- Digital: SVG or high-res PNG
